ABF organised an energetic **Summer Camp** for children in two key areas of Bengaluru, offering sessions in:

- **Karate** — discipline, focus, self-defence
- **Football** — teamwork, fitness, joy
- **Fitness training** — daily routines and habits
- **Computer skills** (for the older cohort) — basics of computers, MS Office, typing

The camp aimed to instill discipline, boost confidence, and promote a healthy lifestyle — while giving kids a fun, structured outlet during the holidays.

It was more than just activity. It was **character-building in action**.
