ABF kicked off an electrifying **Under-12, 7-a-side Football Tournament**, uniting **10 teams** from across Bengaluru in a celebration of talent, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

More than a game, it was a platform to nurture **discipline, passion, and unity** among young players — laying the foundation for a stronger, healthier generation. Sports as a positive alternative to the pressures and pull of substance abuse — central to the **Raah-E-Nijat** thesis.
