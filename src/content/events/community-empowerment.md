ABF led a powerful awareness drive, educating communities on **vital government schemes** while also addressing the **rising threat of substance abuse**. The session empowered attendees with knowledge, resources, and actionable steps — turning awareness into action for a healthier, more informed society.

## Two tracks, one conversation

1. **How to avail government schemes for education and health** — practical, scheme-by-scheme walk-through of what's available and how to apply.
2. **How to fight against drug abuse and protect our youth** — recognising warning signs, accessing rehabilitation, and building community-level prevention.

## Sessions held

- **7 February 2025** — Noorani Masjid, Ilyas Nagar (after Maghrib prayer)
- **16 March 2025** — Lonasters Convention Hall, Billwardahalli
- **23 March 2025** — Trillium PU College, Hegdenagar

The two-track design was deliberate. The same families wrestling with addiction in their household are often unaware of the welfare and counselling support that exists for them. We chose to talk about both in the same room.
