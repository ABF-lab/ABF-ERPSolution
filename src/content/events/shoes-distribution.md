ABF proudly distributed over **1,500 school shoes** to underprivileged children across **Gulbarga, Yadgir, Bidar, Bangalore, Hospet, and Bellary** — ensuring that every step they take toward education is filled with **dignity, confidence, and comfort**.

These are more than just shoes. They are symbols of **hope, equality, and the belief that every child deserves the chance to walk proudly into a better tomorrow**.
