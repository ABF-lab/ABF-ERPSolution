ABF hosted a heartfelt Meet & Greet to celebrate and thank the incredible Sehri providers across Bengaluru who featured on **[bangaloresehri.in](https://bangaloresehri.in)** and selflessly served over **50,000 meals daily** during Ramadan.

These are the silent warriors who ensured no fasting soul went hungry before dawn — turning compassion into action across the city.

The gathering was graced by prominent personalities of Karnataka, acknowledging this grassroots generosity and community spirit. Together, we honoured a movement built not just on food, but on **faith, unity, and unwavering service**.
