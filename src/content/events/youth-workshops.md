ABF conducted three workshop series to nurture young hearts and minds — exploring the wonders of technology, the power of manners, and the harmony between faith and science.

## Robotics

Hands-on, project-based STEM workshops introducing children to the foundations of robotics — circuits, sensors, programming logic, and building things that move.

## Ba Adab Ba Naseeb

A fun, interactive **character-building** programme to help kids build respectful behaviour, manners, and systematic daily routines. Run for boys aged 8 to 14.

## Qur'an & Science

An enlightening session exploring how the Qur'an aligns with modern scientific understanding — bridging revelation and discovery for young learners. Speaker: **Saeed Meer**.

## Why we run these together

Building a generation that is **future-ready** is necessary but not sufficient. We also want them to be **rooted in values** and **guided by purpose**. These workshops do both.
