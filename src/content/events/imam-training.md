In a groundbreaking initiative, ABF — along with **AIMDC** and the **Department of Minorities, Government of Karnataka** — trained over **2,500 Imams, Mutawallis, and Masjid Committee Members** from across the state on the life-changing benefits of government healthcare schemes.

Through **13 powerful sessions** at Haj Bhavan, Bangalore, this mission transformed religious and community leaders into **health warriors** — ready to guide their congregations toward timely care, access to government support, and dignity in healthcare.

## Why this multiplier matters

A masjid Imam in Karnataka speaks to several hundred congregants every week. If even a fraction of those community leaders carry accurate, actionable information about Ayushman Bharat, SAST coverage, or the closest government dialysis centre, the downstream impact is enormous.

This is the **Active Health** bridge model at its most efficient.
