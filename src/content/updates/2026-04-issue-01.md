This issue of *Active Updates* spans the spread of work ABF has done over the last two years — from a 24/7 medical helpline that has saved families ₹3.77 crore, to summer camps, football tournaments, the Sehri Network, and 1,500 pairs of school shoes for children across six Karnataka districts.

## What's new

- **Active Health crossed 1,625 cases** with full-time call operators answering 24×7. [Read more →](/projects/active-health)
- **Mission NoDropOut** delivered a **63.4% pass rate** in SSLC supplementary exams across 23 coaching centres. [Read more →](/projects/mission-nodropout)
- **BangaloreSehri.in** scaled to **100+ providers** and **50,000+ daily Sehri meals** during Ramadan. [Read more →](/projects/bangalore-sehri)
- **Medical & Disaster Equipment Banks** — both initiatives are accepting donations of unused equipment. [Read more →](/projects/medical-equipment-bank)
- **Minority Welfare research** — comparative budget studies and 2,500+ Imams trained on healthcare schemes. [Read more →](/projects/minority-welfare-research)
