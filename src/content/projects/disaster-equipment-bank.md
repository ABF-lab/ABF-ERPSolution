When a flood, a building collapse, or a heatwave hits, the difference between rescue and tragedy is often hours — sometimes minutes. By the time supplies are sourced, the window is closed.

ABF's Disaster Equipment Bank is being built to remove that delay.

## What we are building

- **Power** — portable generators
- **Water rescue** — inflatable boats, life jackets, ropes
- **Search and recovery** — concrete cutters, drills, hard hats, safety boots
- **Personal protection** — masks, gloves, eye protection
- **Coordination kit** — high-vis jackets, communication equipment, first-aid

## Why we are doing this

Our 2024 response to **Tuticorin floods** and 2024 deployment of **Project ZAM ZAM** during Bengaluru's water crisis taught us the same lesson twice: the volunteers and the will exist; the **physical equipment** is the bottleneck.

This bank, once stocked, will be available to ABF teams **and** to partner NGOs in Karnataka and beyond — true to our bridge-not-duplicate model.
