Most home-care equipment sits idle for years after a recovery. Meanwhile, families one neighbourhood over are spending lakhs to rent the same device. The Medical Equipment Bank closes that gap.

## What we accept

- Wheelchairs
- Walkers and walking sticks
- Oxygen cylinders and concentrators
- BiPAP / CPAP machines
- Nebulizers
- Hospital beds
- Water and air mattresses
- Commode stools
- Syringe pumps
- Pulse oximeters and glucometers

If your equipment works, or just needs minor repair, it can save a life.

## How it works

1. **Donate** unused or repairable equipment — call **+91 93640 24365** for pickup in Bengaluru.
2. **Request** equipment if you or someone you know needs it — call our 24/7 Active Health helpline at **+91 93640 24365**.
3. **Return** when you no longer need it, so the next family can use it.

We verify need, sanitise and refurbish equipment between users, and keep a public register of what's currently available.

## Why this matters

The economics are absurd. A used oxygen concentrator depreciates to ~zero in storage; the same machine rents for ₹5,000–₹10,000/month. By circulating equipment, we are quietly returning lakhs to the families who need it most.
