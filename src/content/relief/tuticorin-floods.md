In December 2023, floodwaters in Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu, caused extensive damage that uprooted families, destroyed homes, and disrupted livelihoods. Within days, the Active Bengaluru Foundation mobilised relief from across Bengaluru and drove it to Tuticorin.

## What we delivered

- **2,500 litres** of drinking water
- **4,000** biscuit packets
- **420 litres** of milk
- **650 kg** of rice
- **120 litres** of oil
- **500 pairs** of clothes
- Blankets, ration kits, and medical supplies

## Who delivered

ABF volunteers **Tauseef Ahmed, Mohammed Saif, Sameer Barde, and Mohammed Mehraj** drove the relief in and distributed it on the ground.

In Tauseef Ahmed's words to *The New Indian Express*:

> Our goal was to reach those places that might have been overlooked. We wanted to make a direct impact.

## Collaboration

This effort was carried out in collaboration with **Milli Council KA, Palm Charitable Trust, Project Smile, Beyond Boundaries, Nawayath Club, and Be Charitable**. ABF is grateful to every partner who collaborated.
