01 · Brand Story

We didn't start as an NGO.
We started as neighbours.

Active Bengaluru Foundation was not born in a boardroom. It was born in the middle of a crisis — when a city was struggling to breathe, and a group of ordinary people decided they could not stand by.

During COVID-19, the ABF team ran community kitchens, drove patients to hospitals at 2 AM, tracked down oxygen cylinders, arranged ambulances, and coordinated burials under Mercy Mission and the Emergency Response Team (ERT).

In 2024, we formalised as a Section 8 NGO with 12A and 80G certifications — not because the paperwork mattered, but because the people we serve deserve an organisation that will still be here five years from now.

THE MODEL

"Be the bridge. Don't duplicate."

ABF is the connective tissue between civil society, government, and communities — making every partner stronger.

02 · Mission, Vision & Values

What we stand for.

MISSION

To serve as the bridge between NGOs, government, and underprivileged communities — making every partner stronger.

VISION

A Bengaluru where no citizen falls through the cracks — where the ecosystem of care is as active as the city itself.

01
Show Up.
Before we had a name or letterhead, we were already on the ground. Presence is the foundation of everything we do. When the moment calls, we move.
02
Be the Bridge.
We don't compete with the NGOs around us. We connect them. Collaboration over duplication. Strength through infrastructure, not rivalry.
03
Accountability First.
Goodwill alone isn't enough. Lasting change demands structure, documented impact, and full transparency. The community deserves to know where every rupee goes.
04
People Over Process.
We formalised the promise, not the passion. Rules serve the mission — never the other way around. At the end of every system is a human life we are trying to improve.

  • Use logo on approved backgrounds only (white, cream, yellow, dark ink)

  • Use "ABF" as shorthand in digital UI where space is limited

06 · Voice & Tone

How ABF speaks.

ABF's voice is the voice of neighbours — grounded, honest, urgent when it needs to be, and always human. Our words carry the credibility we earned on the ground.

🔥
Urgent, not alarmist
We communicate need clearly. We don't sensationalise suffering to generate donations, but we don't shy away from gravity.
✦ Write: "This family needs help now. Here's how."
Not: "URGENT: Tragedy strikes!"
🤝
Direct but warm
We speak person-to-person. No corporate-speak, no jargon. Talking to a neighbour, not a benefactor.
✦ Write: "Your donation goes directly to those who need it."
Not: "Contributions are allocated per programme guidelines."
💪
Action-oriented
Every message ends with something the reader can do. We are a movement, not a memo.
✦ Write: "Join us. Volunteer this weekend. Donate ₹500."
Not: "Support for ABF initiatives is appreciated."
📊
Accountable
We share numbers, outcomes, and challenges honestly. Accountability is a brand value, not just compliance.
✦ Write: "₹12.4L raised. 847 patients supported."
Not: "We've helped thousands of people."
🌏
Bengaluru-rooted
We are from this city, for this city. Our language references local geography and community when relevant.
✦ Write: "From Fraser Town to Shivajinagar — ABF is on the ground."
Not vague: "We serve communities across India."
🙏
Dignified always
Beneficiaries are people, not props. We never reduce a person to their suffering. Dignity is non-negotiable.
✦ Write: "A father of two, recovering after surgery."
Never: "Destitute man in critical condition."

08 · Photography Style

Real people. Real moments.

ABF photography is documentary in nature — honest, warm, and always human-first. We never stage suffering. We capture community in action.

✦ Photography Do's
  • Use candid, documentary-style photography
  • Show volunteers and beneficiaries as equals
  • Prefer warm, natural lighting (golden hour)
  • Show Bengaluru as context and backdrop
  • Always obtain written consent before publishing
  • Crop to square or 16:9 for digital consistency
✗ Photography Don'ts
  • Never use stock photography for impact stories
  • Never show children's faces without guardian consent
  • Avoid "poverty porn" — images that reduce dignity
  • No heavy filters or unnatural colour grading
  • Don't use images from non-ABF events without credit
  • Avoid overly polished, corporate-looking shots

10 · Do's & Don'ts

The rules we live by.

These guidelines protect the identity we've built through years of trust. When in doubt, come back to this page.

✦ Brand Identity Do's
  • Always use Archivo Black for headings
  • Always use the approved 8-colour palette only
  • Keep logo clear space equal to the "A" height
  • Use ✦ sparingly — only in Brand Red
  • Use pill-shaped buttons (border-radius: 9999px)
  • Use 24px border radius on all cards
  • Minimum section padding 64px vertical
✦ Communication Do's
  • Write in first person plural ("we", "our")
  • Share specific numbers and outcomes
  • End every message with a clear action
  • Name the city and community you're serving
  • Show gratitude genuinely, not corporately
  • Disclose exactly how donations are used
✗ Brand Identity Don'ts
  • Don't use any font other than Archivo Black + Plus Jakarta Sans
  • Don't use colours outside the approved palette
  • Don't rotate, skew, or distort the logo
  • Don't place logo on busy photographic backgrounds
  • Don't use square or sharp-cornered buttons
  • Don't create new brand colours without approval
✗ Communication Don'ts
  • Don't use NGO jargon ("stakeholders", "beneficiaries")
  • Don't make vague impact claims ("helped thousands")
  • Don't exploit suffering in headlines or imagery
  • Don't sound corporate or transactional
  • Don't over-promise outcomes you can't verify
  • Don't post without consent from people pictured
